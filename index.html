Prototype notes. All data on this page is illustrative, drawn from public reporting, court records, the Human Rights First ICE Flight Monitor, and the UC Berkeley Deportation Data Project. The two datasets cannot be joined at the individual level — DDP has no flight IDs, flight tracking has no manifests — and a production version would say so here, in a full methods note. Tier definitions: Broken — a court, settlement, or inspector general confirmed a violation. Bent — credibly documented, contested or unadjudicated. Rewritten — legal by design: a statute, rule, or memo built to enable the practice. Greyed entries were later stayed, reversed, or dissolved on appeal — the ledger keeps them, because the events still happened.
